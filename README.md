# AtliQ Hardwares Sales & Financial Analytics

An Excel-based sales and financial analysis project for exploring AtliQ Hardwares’ revenue performance, customer growth, product trends, target achievement, and profitability across markets and divisions.

## Project overview

This project organizes sales and target data into reports for reviewing business performance over time. It includes customer net sales comparisons, target performance, product rankings, country and division reports, gross margin analysis, and profit-and-loss summaries.

## Project objectives

- Review net sales performance across fiscal years and customer segments.
- Compare actual sales with targets and identify performance gaps.
- Identify top-performing and bottom-performing products.
- Compare sales across countries, markets, and divisions.
- Review gross margin by quarter and profit-and-loss measures by month and year.
- Explore product introductions and customer performance using report filters.

## Reports included

| Report | Focus |
|---|---|
| Customer Performance Report | Customer net sales by year and year-over-year change |
| Target Performance | 2021 actual performance compared with target |
| Product Reports | Top 10 products, top 5 products, and bottom 5 products |
| Top 5 Countries | Net sales contribution by country |
| Division Report | Sales performance across product divisions |
| Gross Margin by Quarters | Quarterly gross margin percentage by sub-zone |
| Profit & Loss Reports | Monthly, yearly, and market-level P&L views |
| New Products 2021 | New product performance for 2021 |

## Workbook structure

The workbook includes the monthly sales fact table and supporting product, market, date, customer, and target tables. Report sheets and views are included for customer sales and target performance.

## Tools and techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot tables and report filters
- Sales and target variance analysis
- Customer and product performance analysis
- Gross margin and P&L reporting
- Conditional formatting for comparisons and rankings

## How to explore the workbook

1. Open `Sales.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
2. Use the report filters to review sales by region, market, division, customer, or fiscal year where available.
3. Compare the customer, product, country, and division reports.
4. Review the P&L and gross margin reports for profitability trends.
5. Use the supporting data and dimension sheets to understand the report structure.
